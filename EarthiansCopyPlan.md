# Earthians Copy Plan

Source: `Earthians.md` (the 12 Earthian dossiers). Deliverable: presentable,
archival-register copy for each Earthian, suitable for the registry cards and
future detail entries. Every word below is adjusted from the source, not
copied 1:1. Nothing here adds a fact, name, number, or date that is not in
the source.

## Register and ground rules

- Register: the site's "living archive" voice. Factual, composed, restrained.
  No marketing superlatives, no hype. Specifics over adjectives.
- Each entry: one-line lede, then a fact block. Facts stay terse and
  monospace-friendly where they will render as metadata.
- No fabricated detail. Where the source is thin, the copy says so in the
  archive idiom ("few facts filed") rather than inventing.
- Titles stay as filed; titles are proper names of the entries, not copy.

## Entry patterns

Standard order per Earthian:
1. Lede: one or two sentences, who and what.
2. Facts: origin, role, assets/abilities.
3. Affiliations: division or faction, with named members.
4. Note: any Fablea-specific standing (ascension, relationships, duties).

## Shared conventions

- Arrival markers: where the source gives one, keep it in the lede. The
  tidied source places most a year after hmgfan; the dossiers' own lines can
  differ by a few months, so the copy keeps the dossier's number.
- "Ultra-vast powers" is the world's own term; keep it verbatim when it
  appears, do not translate it into bigger adjectives.
- Companion rosters stay as plain name lists, no bold headers, no
  source-attribution parentheticals on the page unless the site wants the
  character's origin flagged.
- Every card can open with the lede and close with the affiliations; the
  fact block sits between. That keeps each card at roughly the same visual
  weight without padding.
